## About

Anne Riley, armed with an MBA, experience in multiple industries, and the mind of a curious philosopher - has been on a lifelong quest to understand how humans logically fit into life's panorama.  Spending much of her time studying the works of Charles Darwin, Stephen Jay Gould, and Michael Rothschild among many others, and immersing herself in the basic knowledge of physics, chemistry, biology, economics, and government - all gave her the foundation for a new way of thinking about humans - and has arrived with her new book to encapsulate these concepts - "The Human Idea - Earths Newest Ecosystem"

Anne Riley is the author of 'The Human Idea - Earths Newest Ecosystem': a book that flips humanity's attention upon itself, and uncovers the essence of what it means to be human. The Ecosystem of Life: Insights into the vast web of life that connects us all, tracing our evolutionary path to understand our place within it.

Anne Riley's narrative is more than a contemplation on our origins; it's a clarion call for our future. ## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Anne Riley is the author of *The Human Idea — Earth's Newest Ecosystem*, an MBA-armed curious philosopher who has spent a lifetime asking how humans are alike and different from other animals—and what that means for living together. On this episode she opens with her mother’s recent death at 102 (Anne is the 11th of 12 children), then traces the theory from a childhood Catholic-school evolution lesson through Stephen Jay Gould, Keynes, Michael Rothschild’s *Bionomics*, and a 2018 congressional campaign that clicked the missing “coordination” piece: government as society’s coordinating system, parallel to the brain in a body. She introduces the ideasphere (a non-physical ecosystem of ideas), the one-sentence survival rule—do whatever you want as long as you don’t harm one another—and practical threads on teaching children that “prime directive,” regulating speech/social media by accurate labeling rather than silencing, the dangers of profit maximization, and the five-word personal practice: identify your values, live them. Published notes point to https://www.thehumanidea.com/, Instagram @anne_riley_ideasphere, and LinkedIn.
