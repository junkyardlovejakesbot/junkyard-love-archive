About

In a podcast where we talk emotions and feelings… musician Kanideis fits like a glove. A talented R&B singer, hard-working visionary and intuitive wordsmith - Kanideis graces our ears in this episode with wisdom and introspective thought.

Kanideis has been working on his performance and musician skills since he was a kid.
