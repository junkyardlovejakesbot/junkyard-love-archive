The Junkyard Love Podcast

Mining the hearts and minds of unorthodox teachers

For the life-long learner who wants honest, human conversations about how we grow, heal, create, and understand ourselves.

These are longform dialogues with artists, thinkers, authors, meditators, psychologists, musicians, creatives, leaders, philosophers, and everyday people with something real to teach. Each episode offers a perspective, story, or insight that tends to land exactly when someone needs it most.

Thoughtful, curious, and quietly life-changing. The Junkyard Love Podcast — for a better life.
