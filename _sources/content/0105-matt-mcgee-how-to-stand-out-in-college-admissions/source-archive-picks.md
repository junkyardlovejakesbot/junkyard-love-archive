## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Matt McGee is an educational consultant, tutor, and college-admissions specialist, and founder of MRM Education (elite tutoring + admissions consultancy; fully virtual since mid-2020). A NACAC member with over a decade of tutoring experience, he helps anxious families navigate an opaque process—raising grades, building three-dimensional applications, and aiming for top / Ivy Plus schools while protecting mental health. On this episode he covers post-pandemic test-optional spikes and tuition sticker shock, how regional officers and committees actually decide, why standing out (and owning failure) matters more than a 4.0 alone, working with neurodivergent students for whom college often still is an option, and how he professionalized tutoring by paying tutors from $80/hour and hiring for charisma/relatability. Background includes high-level (not pro) hockey, Michigan State, and early ESL/private tutoring. Published notes point to https://mrmeducation.com and Instagram @mattmcgee_edconsulting / @mrmeducation.
