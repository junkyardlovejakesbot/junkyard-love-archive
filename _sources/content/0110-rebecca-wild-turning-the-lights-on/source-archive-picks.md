## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Rebecca Wild is a multifaceted wellness visionary and author of *The Will To Live Project*—a holistic health coach with diplomas spanning NLP, Hatha/Chair/Restorative yoga & meditation, counselling, Jungian therapy in coaching, tarot, astrology, past-life regression hypnosis, trauma-informed care, suicide-prevention first aid, peri-natal support, and more. On this episode she traces a 2019 cascade (abusive relationship, cancer/surgeries, postsurgical amnesia, and the death of her soul dog Luna) to a Samaritans call on the floor in the dark—“will you turn the light on”—then a solo Scotland trip that reopened spirituality. She shares rock-bottom “one more try” hope, dating-yourself practices, phone boundaries (including one present meal a day), neurodivergent-friendly meditation (including affirmation tracks on Insight Timer), breathwork for anxiety, and needle-movers from fasting to intentional psychedelic work—while insisting meditation can reach the same places without it. Published notes point to https://www.rebeccawild.org/, Instagram @rebeccaaaawild, YouTube The Sail Away Girl, Insight Timer, Linktree, and the Amazon book link.
