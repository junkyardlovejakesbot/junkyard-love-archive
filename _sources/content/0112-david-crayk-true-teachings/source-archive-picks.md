## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

David Crayk is a retired teacher, poet, writer, bodybuilder, disc golfer, “kid whisperer,” spiritual adviser, and traveler whose lifetime motto is “dare to be different.” Jacob’s high-school drama/theatre teacher at Kelso, he built a judgment-free safe-place classroom (no textbook—self-created games and scenarios), invented the beyond-rubric “five,” and kept lifelong ties with hundreds of former students. On this episode he traces the path from college dropout and military single dad (carpet cleaning by night) to the substitute-teaching click that became a 30+ year calling; talks depression, goals made visual, and 38 years of marriage; frames service, listening, and a “matrix of influence” as how pieces of a teacher ripple outward; and connects creativity, the muse, and not bargaining with the inner guiding voice to scripture study, nature/disc golf, and fitness.
