## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Julie Hoyle is a spiritual coach, author, teacher, and high-school art & design educator who supports people through kundalini awakenings and recognizing who they are beyond the personal story. In 1989—after a lifetime of lucid dreaming and out-of-body experiences, while living in the Bahamas—she received shaktipat in a lucid dream from Bhagawan Nityananda (Om Namah Shivaya; crown-to-cell energetic transmission), after which her life “never [was] the same.” Later ashram years opened intense shadow work (including Irina Tweedie’s *The Chasm of Fire*); around 2016 she dropped formal seeking practices to rest in the silence prior to thought. Since ~2007 she has written books and guided journals (including *An Awakened Life* and shadow-work titles) “downloaded” rather than ego-driven, hosts Talking True (YouTube interviews on awakenings / NDEs / mystical experience), and teaches intuitive art. Published notes point to her Amazon author store, Talking True on YouTube, Understanding Kundalini sessions (https://juliehoyle.org/private-sessions), and Linktree (https://linktr.ee/juliehoyleofficial).
