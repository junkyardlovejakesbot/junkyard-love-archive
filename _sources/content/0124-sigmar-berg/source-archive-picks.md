## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Sigmar Berg is an Austrian-born architect, photographer, artist, and former fashion designer whose life shifted after one guided Aqua Journey breathwork session played through live speakers on 528 Hz — the “love frequency” / miracle tone. Published notes say he created Lovetuner, a small flute-like device you wear and breathe through so the exhale becomes one calibrated 528 Hz note; he wrote *One Journey*, founded a California nonprofit working with schools and veterans, and brings that love-and-peace mission to Junkyard Love. On the episode he frames Lovetuner as a conscious breathing break and “shortcut to meditation,” contrasts living from the head vs the heart (hearts curious; intellect separates), teaches slow-exhale / audible-feedback breathwork to leave fight-or-flight, recommends morning “tone of the day,” emergency, and night cortisol practices, demystifies 528 Hz in nature (bees, chlorophyll) and older musical scales vs 440 Hz concert pitch, ties authenticity to wholeness, describes rising Schumann resonance as a call to raise frequency, and closes with trust-your-intuition / keep-it-light.
