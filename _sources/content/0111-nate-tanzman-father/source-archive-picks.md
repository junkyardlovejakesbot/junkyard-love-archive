## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Nate Tanzman is a husband, father (three kids under four), stunt-bike rider, motivational speaker, videographer, and content-agency entrepreneur (“Nasty Nate”) who spent a decade pouring all time/money/energy into stunt riding before fatherhood and faith reoriented him. On this episode—his fourth Junkyard Love appearance—he traces a bike that interrupted suicidal despair, the death-and-rebirth of the old “Nasty Nate” identity, and a scripture-rooted fatherhood ethic: model a man his son would be proud of and his daughter would marry; treat the weight that wants to “end the game” as purpose and responsibility; invest in lineage over career (“feel how wide my son’s rib cage is today”); raise warriors with discernment or watch the lineage fail; and do the no-credit work of planting fruit you may never see. He talks fulfillment over happiness, cold-plunge all-in commitment, money as time/tool, not over-crediting “the enemy,” heart-vs-image accountability, and a prayer that removed his biggest clients after his best money month.
