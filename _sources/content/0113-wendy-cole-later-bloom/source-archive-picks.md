## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Wendy Cole is a Transition Mentor, coach, speaker, and co-host of Demystifying the Transgender Journey (with Lynn Murphy) who completed her own transition around age 67 after starting to live authentically in 2015. A former tech consultant (18 years), she uses mindfulness, visioning, journaling, Joe Dispenza–style belief work, therapy, and hypnotherapy to help clients confront limiting beliefs and align outside with inside. On this episode she traces childhood knowing, parental rejection at age 10, decades of secrecy while married and raising a family, the January 2015 therapy breakthrough (name “Wendy,” therapist Stephanie), divorce after HRT boundaries, health recovery from living in truth, public-speaking fear → Toastmasters, a feel-first leap to Rhode Island, and coaching tools from supermarket “life tests” to reconstructing thought patterns so transition needn’t be long and hard. Her mission is to humanize being born transgender for cisgender society and grow informed allies. Published notes point to wendycolegtm.net, LinkedIn, YouTube @wendycole212, and the Demystifying podcast (site / YouTube / Podbean).
