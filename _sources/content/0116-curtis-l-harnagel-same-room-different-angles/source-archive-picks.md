## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Curtis L. Harnagel is a creative polymath — director, consultant, and producer moving between live entertainment, multimedia, dance, art, and nonprofit worlds — who coined GESAMTCURTISWERK (“Total Work of Curtis,” from Gesamtkunstwerk) and the tagline “Turning Dreams Into Entertainment.” Published notes place him with an RTF (Radio, Television, and Film) degree, lighting grand productions like Phantom of the Opera, award-winning dance work, massive live events, and mentoring creative teams; Jacob met him on-site as Music and Staging Director at a fashion-show fundraiser (DIFFA Dallas) while Jacob DJed. On this episode he traces educators who opened alternate viewpoints (university documentary class, Tim Hood’s media-tech lab, Mrs. Randolph’s socially conscious classroom), live-event communication under an hourglass clock (rephrase instead of getting louder; trust now / explain later; nothing’s personal), kindness vs niceness and benevolence, “your emergency is not my emergency,” finishing the show without berating a crew already beating itself up, and insight as the little extra you don’t yet know. Published notes point to gesamtcurtiswerk.com, Instagram/YouTube @gesamtcurtiswerk, Bold Journey / Voyage Dallas / Canvas Rebel interviews, DIFFA links, and Curtis’s official Ep 0116 Spotify playlist.
