## Short guest bio (from episode speech + published link)

Bobby Barrz is a Pacific Northwest rapper, studio engineer, and the CEO & creator of Big Bet Studios (also framed as a brand / entertainment company). On this episode he talks about coming into music later—he says he started rapping at 29—building engineering/mixing/mastering/producing skills, releasing early work like Feel Blessed and the They Ain't Ready EP, and treating music as therapy, brand, and legacy. He shares perspectives on personal responsibility, learning from mistakes (“watching game film”), marriage and aspiring fatherhood, staying prepared to answer the call, and hosting an open mic (Day Shift) at Darnell’s bar in Vancouver, Washington.
