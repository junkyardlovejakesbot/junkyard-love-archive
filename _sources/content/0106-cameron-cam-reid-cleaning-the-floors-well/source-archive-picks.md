On this episode he traces his path from childhood anger and a first-love introduction to self-help, through scrubbing machine-shop linoleum tile-by-tile (his first meditation: stoned wax-on/wax-off breath counts), to growing up with much older entrepreneur parents (dad born 1935, mom 1955), millennial identity mixed with older-generation morals, and a deep pull toward faith, trust, and community without needing one church’s answers. From Moab he riffs with Jacob on Austin as a warm, edge-pushing hub and brainstorms weekly “containers” for connection, storytelling, and holding space. Published notes list no guest links; About emphasizes medicinal psychedelics, meditation, breathwork, intentional movement, and expressing love when it’s hardest.
