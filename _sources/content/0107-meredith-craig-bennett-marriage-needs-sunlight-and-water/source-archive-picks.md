On this episode she shares their 2020 corporate-exit story—same company, office closing, her dad’s cancer diagnosis that Christmas giving them “last Christmas” perspective to leave without a fully baked plan (“jumped out of the plane… build a parachute on the way down”). Published notes point to https://roadoflifecoaching.com/, Instagram @meredithandcraig, their YouTube channel, and Facebook.

Craig Bennett is a marriage and relationship coach (with Meredith) who frames their work around relationship pillars and a 200% marriage mindset (each partner 100% responsible—not 50/50 scorekeeping). On this episode he covers the six-week real-estate business they hated and shuttered when it chipped at their marriage bedrock, stumbling into personal growth, then stage talks that became coaching; being married vs building a marriage; marriage as a life “cheat code”; tactical invite/check-in/“let them in” habits; non-negotiable hugs; and walk-and-talk for harder conversations. They also host the bite-sized Road of Life Podcast and are most active on Instagram @meredithandcraig.
