## Short guest bio (from episode speech + published About/link)

Swami Nityananda is the spiritual teacher of the Awake Yoga Meditation community in Baltimore (also online), consecrated by / successor to Swami Shankarananda, who asked her to teach kriya yoga after he left the body in 2014. On this episode she describes growing up in Washington state as Juniper Ellis with a natural sense of non-separation from the natural world; community meditations (Sundays in person; Tuesdays and Fridays online); one-on-one kriya practice (about 20 minutes morning and evening); and teachings on joy and freedom in daily life. Published notes also identify her as Professor of English at Loyola University (PhD, Vanderbilt), author of Tattooing the World: Pacific Designs in Print and Skin, and author of Awake: The Yoga of Pure Awareness / Awake Awareness; links point to awakeyogameditation.org and the book on Amazon.
