Fran Frye is a sex educator and counselor in Charleston. She helps men understand themselves sexually so they can have a more fulfilling life — in bed and outside it. That includes men who watch more porn than they meant to, feel gone from their bodies during sex, keep a secret sex life, or sit in the shower afterward thinking why did I do that again. She is a rape survivor, an athlete, and a death doula.

Fran and I talk about the years she disappeared from her own body after she was raped in college, and how movement brought her back. We talk about why you can be hard when you are actually angry, scared, or just flooded — and why getting off does not answer what the body was asking. We get into the shame after cheating and after masturbation, the missing words for what you actually want in bed, porn as both a relief and a stand-in for sex you are not having, how she took her power back after harm, and the intimacy nobody wants to name when someone is dying.

Her line from the conversation: you don't have a sex problem. You have a nervous system problem.
