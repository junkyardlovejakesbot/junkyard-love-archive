## Short guest bio (from episode speech + published links)

Jerry Fu is a Conflict Resolution Coach who facilitates leadership workshops and previously worked as a pharmacist. He completed a Biology degree at Rice University and pharmacy school at the University of Tennessee-Memphis, and now helps clients — including Asian-American leaders — handle conflict at work, in culture, and within themselves via Adapting Leaders. On this episode he talks with Jacob about fear of conflict, people-pleasing, mentorship, passive-aggressiveness, humility, and more — pointing listeners to https://www.adaptingleaders.com/ and his LinkedIn.
