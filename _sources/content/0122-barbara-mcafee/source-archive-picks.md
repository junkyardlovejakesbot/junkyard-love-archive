## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Barbara McAfee is a voice whisperer, author, singer, and playful catalyst for authentic expression who has spent more than three decades helping people shed vocal habits shaped by old stories and step into voices that feel like home. Published notes frame her as master voice coach and bestselling author of *Full Voice* and *Vocal Intelligence*, with two TEDx talks (including the widely viewed “Bringing Your Full Voice to Life”), work with leaders, organizations, international educators, community singing movements, coach trainings, and her beloved Morning Star Singers / comfort hospice choir. On the episode she maps the “vocal girdle” and Five Elements (Earth, Fire, Water, Metal, Air), teaches character/archetype play as the “secret sauce,” traces her path from a Minneapolis workshop through mentors Shaul Ryan and Richard Armstrong to training ~75 coaches and co-teaching with nephew Travis, celebrates group singing as mycelial connection (including abdicated TEDx leadership), offers daily practices (laugh like a hairy oaf, voice your yawns, read children’s books), reframes code-switching and the water/heart voice, and closes toward silence-after-song, living/dying retreats, and books/courses. Published guest links: barbaramcafee.com, TEDx Full Voice, Full Voice / Vocal Intelligence (Amazon & Audible), Bandcamp music, Instagram @barbaramcafeesings.
