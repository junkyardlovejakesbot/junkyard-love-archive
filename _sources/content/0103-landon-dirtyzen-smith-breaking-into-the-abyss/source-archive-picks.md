## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Landon Smith — better known as Dirtyzen — is a creative / spiritual artist who interlaces chillstep (a slower, relaxed dubstep sub-genre) with talks from teachers like Ram Dass and Alan Watts, sharing mixes and contemplative media as a form of karma yoga and Dharma. On this episode he traces a ~2016 spiritual awakening (heartbreak → Eastern philosophy online, including figures like Ki Fresco / Vishuddha Das), using tarot for present-moment clarity with discernment around algorithmic feeds, and psychedelic experiences (LSD, mushrooms) that surfaced fear/aloneness and propelled his path — including an Osho-era chillstep mix made while contemplating “I don’t exist.” Conversation also covers spiritual bypassing, toxic positivity, intellectual vs embodied knowing, the evolving “path of the guru” in a YouTube age, and recognizing God / true Self rather than placing the divine only outside oneself. Published notes point to YouTube @Dirtyzen, Instagram @Dirtyzen, and a favorite SoundCloud mix (https://on.soundcloud.com/WUqKdc5jYU5JaQ2x8).
