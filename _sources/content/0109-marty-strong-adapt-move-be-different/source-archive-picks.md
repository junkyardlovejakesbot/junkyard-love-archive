## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Marty Strong is a retired Navy SEAL officer (10 years enlisted + 10 years as an officer), former UBS portfolio manager, counterterrorism returnee after 9/11, and currently CEO of a middle-market healthcare company. He serves on the boards of BEST Robotics, Inc. and Metal Raptor, LLC, is president of BEST MindLab, holds a BBA and MBA, and is a certified Master Blackbelt in Lean Six Sigma. Author of nine published novels and three business books (*Be Nimble*, *Be Visionary*, *Be Different*—with a 10th novel shopping to agents), his articles on leadership and creativity have appeared on CNBC online, Fast Company, CEO World, and Chief Executive Magazine. On this episode he frames a life of “zigzag” opportunity windows (Nebraska → mistaken orders into Coronado SEAL training → money management → contractor “business doctor”), and teaches open-mindedness + intellectual humility + risk-taking, comfort with change (universe abhors stasis), discipline as tiny repeated behaviors, pull vs push motivation, military/career reinvention via an apprentice mindset, freedom of the mind, and why *Be Different* asks readers to paint outside the lines. Published notes point to https://martystrongbenimble.com/.
