## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Megan Hawkins is a co-founder and support/integration coach at BetterU Psychedelic Therapy (at-home ketamine via telemedicine, with a Los Angeles brick-and-mortar in the works alongside partner Derek Du Chesne). Once an international model, she was shot at 19 in a nightclub assassination attempt, then spent years overdiagnosed/overmedicated while cycling through modeling, mortgages/real estate, and a hard rock-bottom before discovering ketamine therapy in 2021. On this episode she walks newcomers through BetterU’s process (brain box, dosing, peer monitor, journaling, music, screening) and the difference between therapeutic set/setting and street/horse-tranquilizer myths. Published notes point to https://www.betterucare.com/, Instagram @betterucare, and YouTube @ketaminetherapy.
