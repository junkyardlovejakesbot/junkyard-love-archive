## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Nate Begle is a voice actor, puppeteer, storyteller, and sound/audio producer with 25+ years bringing characters and stories to life across stage, screen, voiceover, puppetry, theme-park entertainment, national commercials, animation/video games, dubbing, live announcing, and 30+ audiobooks (including James Patterson titles). Published notes place him with James Patterson, The Jim Henson Company, Walt Disney Entertainment, and Sesame Street; he prefers working “behind the mic or below the frame.” On this episode he traces a childhood Fraggle Rock “light switch” into library self-education, Kids on the Block, musical theater + digital media degrees, an Amtrak move to NYC (Die Hard the Puppet Musical → Henson/Sesame rooms), mentor Tyler Bunch opening the VO door, Meisner’s “behaving truthfully under imaginary circumstances,” Mick Wingert’s “irreducible minimum” (storytelling), coaching actors from age 4 to 92, a thousand-show blacklight tour, and advice to pick one focus and ask people who already do the work. Published notes point to IMDb, natebegle.com, YouTube @NateBegle, and LinkedIn.
