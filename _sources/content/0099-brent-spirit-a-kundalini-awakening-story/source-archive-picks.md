## Short guest bio (from episode speech + published About/link)

Brent Spirit (first name Brent; “Spirit” is a branding alias used for spiritual work, separate from his photography under his given name) is a spiritual teacher who hosts The Spiritual Awakening Show and shares on YouTube/Instagram @BrentSpirit and BrentSpirit.com. On this episode he describes ~15 years of unintentional kundalini / spiritual awakening—childhood dissociation and high-school depression, Eckhart Tolle and witnessing practice, a spontaneous spine movement at ~15, a 2012 top-down “Zen sickness” / no-self opening in Toronto, years of embodiment and unconditional self-love, then a major root-to-crown rising (2015) during a psilocybin session, followed by purification and integration into the heart. Published About notes also frame him as helping others through similar processes via his channel and podcast.
