## Short guest bio (from episode speech + published About/link)

Kevin Foreman is President of Black Sheep Business Consulting (bsbcon.com), where he and his team help turn ideas into business plans. On this episode he describes about five and a half years of sobriety after a physical altercation at a Whitecaps / soccer supporters’ section led to assault charges, a court order not to drink, AA men’s meetings, anxiety therapy, and a spiritual path (Taoism themes and his own faith in Jesus). Earlier he aspired to play professional rugby, worked in his dad’s property-services business, traveled widely in his early twenties, then worked in utilities. He still attends the same Monday-night 8 PM men’s AA meeting and continues therapy; published notes point listeners to bsbcon.com.
