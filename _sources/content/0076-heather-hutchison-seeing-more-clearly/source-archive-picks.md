## Short guest bio (from episode speech + published About/links)

Heather Hutchison is an award-winning singer/songwriter and the author of Holding On by Letting Go: A Memoir. Blind since birth and having struggled with mental illness from a young age, she educates on disability and mental health through her music and writing; she has lived and taught English in Latin America. On Junkyard Love she talks with Jacob about anxiety, hospitalization, meditation, purpose, holding on through dark moments, and the state of mental health after COVID-19 — links: https://books.heather-hutchison.com/, Spotify, YouTube HHutchisonMusic, Instagram @heatherhutchisonmusic, and Amazon ebook.
