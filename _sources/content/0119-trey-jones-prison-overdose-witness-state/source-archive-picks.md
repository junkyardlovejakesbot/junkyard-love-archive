## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Published notes frame him as founder of From Prison To Purpose / Personal Authority Coach under PrisonToPurpose444, doing 1:1 interventions to dismantle victim identity and rebuild authority; a proud father with his partner and son in the Great Northwest, whom Jacob met at a forest gathering of healers and artists. On the episode he walks through his rising ritual (meditation, gratitude-as-energy, mobility, cold finish), teaches victim mentality as inverted narcissism, willpower as a muscle, Ken Wilber’s witness (“the true you is the witness behind it all”), prison-as-mindset after a roofing injury pushed him into purposeful social media, healing as becoming someone the past can no longer control, Christ consciousness / Schumann waking, empathy with standards, and a closing charge to chase your higher calling.
