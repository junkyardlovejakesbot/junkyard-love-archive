## Short guest bio (from published About + episode speech + published links)

Ravinder Taylor is a UK-certified psychotherapist and clinical hypnotherapist (National College of Hypnosis and Psychotherapy) with a BSc in microbiology and 30+ years researching human behavior, subconscious programming, and self-empowerment. She has co-authored 200+ personal-development audio programs and two books, working extensively with New York Times bestselling author (and husband) Eldon Taylor on InnerTalk research, editing, and program development; she authored Mind Training: The Science of Self-Empowerment. On this episode she traces a path-lab hypnosis demo that led to her own hypnotherapy (unlocking a childhood near-drowning trauma), three years of formal training, and healing rheumatoid arthritis after her second child—combining InnerTalk “accelerated healing” for 15 months, placebo framing, and a short CMO course, then remaining RA-med-free for 25 years. She unpacks optimism research, bedtime breathing/forgiveness/gratitude habits, free will as something you take, media compliance priming, why fluff-free “why + how” beats empty manifesting, and smiling as practical medicine.
