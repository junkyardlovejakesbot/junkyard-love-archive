In this episode of The Junkyard Love Podcast, we meet Wendy Cole, a 67-year-old Transition Mentor who completed her own transition at the age of 67. Wendy shares her inner perspective from her years as a married man and father while grappling deeply with identity, societal expectations, and secrets that visited her sleep. Wendy shares stories of how she embraced her true self later in life, and we discuss her intensive internal exploration, self-work, her time in therapy, friends along the way, meditation, Joe Dispenza, reshaping limiting beliefs, creating new identities, and more.

Wendy invited me to think differently, and I had a great time exploring the heart and mind of this unorthodox teacher. Her story offers us deep insight into the human being's profound ability for change - at any age.
